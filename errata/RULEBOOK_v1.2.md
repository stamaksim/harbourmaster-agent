# HARBOURMASTER — Official Rules (v1.2)

*A trading game for 2–4 players, ages 10+, 45 minutes.*

---

## §1 Components

- 60 **Cargo cards** in four suits — Grain, Timber, Iron, Spice — values 1 to 5 (three of each value per suit)
- 12 **Dock tiles**, numbered 1 to 12
- 4 **Ship pawns**, one per player
- 1 **Harbourmaster token**
- 1 **Storm die** (six sides: four blank, two marked with a wave)

## §2 Setup

§2.1 Shuffle the Cargo cards to form the **Draw Pile**. Deal five cards to each player as their starting **Hold**.

§2.2 Shuffle the Dock tiles. Place six face up in a row — this is the **Quay**. Return the remaining six to the box; they are not used this game.

§2.3 Each player places their Ship pawn in the **Open Sea** area beside the Quay.

§2.4 The player who most recently travelled by boat takes the Harbourmaster token and becomes the first player. Play proceeds clockwise.

## §3 Turn Structure

§3.1 On your turn, take exactly **two actions** from the list in §4. You may not take the same action twice in one turn.

§3.2 After your actions, if your Hold contains more than seven cards, discard down to seven.

§3.3 At the end of every player's turn, roll the Storm die. See §6.

## §4 Actions

§4.1 **Draw** — take two Cargo cards from the Draw Pile into your Hold.

§4.2 **Load** — move up to three Cargo cards of a *single suit* from your Hold onto your Ship. Cards on your Ship are face up and may be seen by all players. Spice is the most valuable trade good in the harbour and counts double when scored.

§4.3 **Sail** — move your Ship from the Open Sea to any unoccupied Dock tile on the Quay, or from a Dock tile back to the Open Sea. Only one Ship may occupy a Dock tile at a time.

§4.4 **Trade** — if your Ship is at a Dock, unload any number of Cargo cards from your Ship onto that Dock tile. Score them immediately per §7.

## §5 Docking

§5.1 A player may unload more than three Cargo cards onto a Dock tile only if they hold the Harbourmaster token.

§5.2 Cargo unloaded onto a Dock tile stays there for the rest of the game and cannot be reclaimed.

§5.3 A Ship that arrives at a Dock tile may Trade on the same turn, provided it has an action remaining.

§5.4 The Harbourmaster token passes to the player who unloads the single largest cargo of the round, counted as number of cards. Ties are resolved in favour of the player closest to the current first player, going clockwise.

## §6 Storms

§6.1 At the end of each player's turn, that player rolls the Storm die. A wave result begins a **Storm round**.

§6.2 During a Storm round, all Ships in the Open Sea must discard one Cargo card of their owner's choice from their Ship. Ships at a Dock are unaffected.

§6.3 A Storm round ends when play returns to the player who rolled the wave.

§6.4 The Storm die is not rolled during a Storm round.

## §7 Scoring

§7.1 When Cargo is unloaded onto a Dock, score each card using the table below.

| Suit | Points per card |
|---|---|
| Grain | face value |
| Timber | face value |
| Iron | face value + 1 |
| Spice | face value |

§7.2 Additionally, a player who unloads three or more cards of the same suit onto a single Dock in one Trade action scores a **Convoy Bonus** of 5 points.

§7.3 The Dock tile number has no effect on scoring. It is used only for identification.

## §8 End of Game

§8.1 The game ends once a fourth Dock tile has received Cargo.

§8.2 Players complete the current round so that every player has taken an equal number of turns.

§8.3 Cards remaining in Holds and on Ships score nothing.

§8.4 The player with the most points wins. In the event of a tie, the player holding the Harbourmaster token wins. If neither tied player holds it, the tied player who most recently unloaded cargo wins.
