Claude Code's first version of agent/rulebook.py captured bare container headings (§2, §3, §4... §8 — the ones with no sub-number, just a title like "## §4 Actions") as dictionary entries with no real rule content. I caught this by running the parser and inspecting the actual output against RULEBOOK.md, not from the agent's own summary of what it built. Fixed by asking it to skip storing an entry when there's no body text beyond the heading itself.
